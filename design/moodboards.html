Pocket CEO brand directions

Three ways the product could look. Each board shows the palette, the two typefaces, and two things an owner actually receives: the morning brief and the cover of the year-end package. Pick one (or say what to steal from another), and it becomes one CSS file every email, PDF and page uses.

Ledger
