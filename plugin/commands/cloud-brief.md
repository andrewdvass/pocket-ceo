---
description: Move your morning brief to the cloud so it arrives even when your computer is off
---

Follow `cloud-routine.md` in the `daily-brief` skill folder. Check the four requirements first and
explain any that are missing in plain words (offer to turn on the online backup if that's the gap).
Then copy the brief into their backup, create the routine, run it once, and confirm with them that
the email arrived. If they had an on-this-computer brief task, remove it only after the cloud one works,
so they never get two briefs or none.
