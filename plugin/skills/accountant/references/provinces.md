# Province and territory quick reference

Last checked: **2026-10-14**. Rates below matched the January 2026 rate tables (see `../../../SOURCES.md`).

**Verify every number with an official source before quoting it.** Rates and thresholds change.
Federal: canada.ca/en/revenue-agency. Quebec: revenuquebec.ca.

## Federal basics (all provinces)
- **GST:** 5%. Must register once taxable sales pass **$30,000** in any four consecutive calendar quarters
  (the "small supplier" threshold). Registering earlier is allowed and lets you claim input tax credits.
- **Sole proprietor tax return:** filing deadline June 15; **any balance owing is due April 30.** Interest runs on anything unpaid after April 30 even if they file by June 15.
- **Instalments:** generally required when net tax owing is over **$3,000** (over $1,800 in Quebec)
  in the current year and either of the two previous years. Due March 15, June 15, Sept 15 and Dec 15.
  CRA sends reminders.
- **CPP:** self-employed people pay both the employee and employer portions on net business income.
- **Corporations:** T2 due 6 months after year end; tax balance generally due 2 months after year end
  (3 months for many small Canadian-controlled private corporations).
- **Records:** keep 6 years.

## Sales tax by province (rate charged depends on where the customer is)
| Province / territory | Sales tax | Who collects the provincial part |
|---|---|---|
| Alberta | GST 5% only | — |
| British Columbia | GST 5% + PST 7% | BC Ministry of Finance (separate PST registration) |
| Saskatchewan | GST 5% + PST 6% | Saskatchewan Finance (separate PST registration) |
| Manitoba | GST 5% + RST 7% | Manitoba Finance (separate RST registration) |
| Ontario | HST 13% | CRA (one HST account) |
| Quebec | GST 5% + QST 9.975% | Revenu Québec (handles both) |
| New Brunswick | HST 15% | CRA |
| Nova Scotia | HST 14% (reduced from 15% on April 1, 2025) | CRA |
| Prince Edward Island | HST 15% | CRA |
| Newfoundland and Labrador | HST 15% | CRA |
| Yukon, Northwest Territories, Nunavut | GST 5% only | — |

Notes:
- Provincial sales taxes (BC, SK, MB) have their own registration rules and exemptions, and many
  services are exempt from them. Check the province's site for the specific service.
- Many health services are GST/HST-exempt (for example, services by certain regulated health
  professionals). Exemptions depend on the profession and the service: verify for the owner's profession.

## Payroll extras by province (only matters once they have employees)
| Province | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Ontario | Employer Health Tax above an annual payroll exemption; WSIB coverage |
| British Columbia | Employer Health Tax above an exemption; WorkSafeBC |
| Manitoba | Health and Post-Secondary Education Tax Levy above an exemption; WCB Manitoba |
| Quebec | QPP instead of CPP, QPIP, Health Services Fund, CNESST; payroll filed with Revenu Québec too |
| Newfoundland and Labrador | Health and Post-Secondary Education Tax above an exemption |
| All others | Workers' compensation board registration (e.g. WCB Alberta) and federal CPP/EI |

## Personal income tax
Every province has its own brackets on top of federal ones. For an estimate, use the owner's
province's current-year combined rates from an official or reputable calculator, and say it's an estimate.
