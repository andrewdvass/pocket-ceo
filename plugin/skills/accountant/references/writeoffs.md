# Common write-offs for small businesses (federal rules, apply everywhere)

Last checked against official and professional sources: **2026-10-14** (see `../../../SOURCES.md`).

The test for any expense: **was it spent to earn business income, and is it reasonable?** Keep the
receipt plus a one-line note of the business purpose. Personal portions are never deductible.

| Expense | Rule of thumb | Keep |
|---|---|---|
| Phone and internet | Business-use % only (e.g. 70%) | Bill + a short usage estimate |
| Home office | Only if it's the main place of business, or used only for business and regularly to meet clients. Deduct the workspace % of rent/mortgage interest, utilities, insurance, property tax. Can't create or increase a loss (carry forward instead) | Floor-plan % calculation |
| Vehicle | Business km ÷ total km × costs (gas, insurance, repairs, lease or depreciation). Commuting to a regular workplace is personal | **A logbook** (a full year, or a representative 3-month sample after a base year) |
| Meals and entertainment with clients | 50% deductible | Receipt + who and why |
| Software, subscriptions, apps | 100% if for the business | Invoice |
| Advertising and marketing | 100% (some limits on foreign media) | Invoices |
| Professional fees (accountant, lawyer, consultants) | 100% if business related | Invoices |
| Training and courses | Deductible if it maintains or upgrades skills for the current business | Receipt |
| Professional dues and licences | Deductible | Receipt |
| Equipment and computers | **Current law:** claimed over time as capital cost allowance (CCA); computers are generally class 50, vehicles class 10 or 10.1. **Proposed (announced Sept 15, 2026, not law yet):** the "Productivity Mega Deduction" would let most depreciable property acquired on or after Sept 15, 2026 be written off 100% in the first year, for corporations and for sole proprietors, with no dollar cap. For individuals it can't create or increase a business loss. Excluded: most buildings, goodwill and licences, and passenger vehicles and many vans/pickups (classes 10/10.1) unless assembled in Canada and not used before. Always say whether it's enacted yet, and check canada.ca/finance before quoting | Invoice + date it was first used |
| Contractors / subcontractors | Deductible; may need T4A slips for payments over $500 for services | Invoices, contracts |
| Bank fees, payment processing, interest on business loans | Deductible | Statements |
| Insurance (business liability, professional) | Deductible | Policy invoice |

Never deductible: personal clothing (unless a uniform or protective gear), fines and penalties, most
club memberships, personal travel, your own wages as a sole proprietor.

When an expense is mixed (part business, part personal), always give the percentage method and the
record that supports it. That turns a scary-sounding question into a Low-risk answer.
