# Commercial lease: clause library

Commercial tenants have far less protection than residential tenants: what's in the lease is mostly
what applies, so negotiation matters. Most landlords expect 3 to 6 change requests from a small tenant.
Rule of thumb: anything over 3 years or with a personal guarantee is worth a lawyer's one-hour review
before signing (after you've done the full review below).

| Clause | Normal | Red flag (rate High) | Ask for (paste-ready) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Term and renewal | 3 to 5 years, with an option to renew at "fair market rent" | Long term with no renewal option, or renewal at landlord's sole discretion | "The Tenant has one option to renew for a further term of [3] years on [6] months' written notice, at fair market rent, determined by arbitration if the parties do not agree." |
| Early exit | Rare, but small tenants can get one | No way out for 5+ years | "The Tenant may terminate this Lease after the [second] anniversary on [six (6)] months' written notice and payment of [two (2)] months' base rent." |
| Base rent increases | Fixed steps, or CPI | Fixed % above inflation each year (5% a year is about 22% over 5 years) | "Base rent increases on each anniversary by the lesser of [3]% and the increase in the Consumer Price Index for [province]." |
| Additional rent (TMI / CAM) | Tenant pays share of taxes, maintenance, insurance, with yearly statements | Uncapped, no audit right, management fee over 15% | "Controllable operating costs will not increase by more than [5]% a year. The Tenant may review the Landlord's records for the operating cost statement once a year." |
| Repairs | Tenant: interior and its own fixtures. Landlord: roof, structure, building systems | Tenant responsible for roof, structure, or replacing HVAC | "The Landlord is responsible for repairs and replacements to the roof, foundation, structure and base building systems, including replacement of HVAC equipment." |
| Personal guarantee | Common for new businesses | Unlimited and for the whole term and renewals | "The Indemnifier's liability is limited to [12] months of base rent and ends after the [second] anniversary if the Tenant is not in default." |
| Use clause | Describes the business broadly enough to grow | So narrow that adding a service breaches the lease | "The Premises may be used for [describe] and any related or ancillary lawful business purpose." |
| Exclusivity | Retail tenants often get it | None, in a plaza where a competitor could move in next door | "The Landlord will not lease space in the [plaza] to another business whose primary use is [describe]." |
| Assignment and subletting | Landlord's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld | Landlord may refuse for any reason, or takes the profit | "The Landlord's consent to an assignment or sublease will not be unreasonably withheld or delayed. No consent is needed for a transfer to a corporation controlled by the Tenant." |
| Relocation | Rare in small plazas | Landlord can move you on short notice | Delete it, or "...only to comparable premises, at the Landlord's cost, including moving, fit-up and signage, with [120] days' notice." |
| Fixturing period and improvements | Rent-free fit-up period (1 to 3 months), landlord work listed | No rent-free period; you restore the space at the end, including landlord's work | "The Tenant has a rent-free fixturing period of [60] days. At the end of the Term the Tenant is not required to remove leasehold improvements made with the Landlord's approval." |
| Demolition / redevelopment | Long notice and compensation | Termination on 60 to 90 days' notice for redevelopment | "The Landlord may terminate for demolition only after the [third] year, on [12] months' notice, and will repay the unamortized cost of the Tenant's improvements." |
| Default and cure | Written notice and time to fix (10 days for rent, 30 for other breaches) | Immediate termination, no notice | "The Tenant will have [10] days after written notice to cure a monetary default and [30] days to cure any other default." |

## Dates to put in `memory/promises.md`
Commencement and rent start dates, each rent step, the renewal-option notice deadline (count back from
the end of the term), insurance certificate renewal, and the end of any guarantee.
