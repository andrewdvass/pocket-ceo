# Independent contractor agreement: clause library

Two uses: the owner hiring a contractor, or the owner working as one for a bigger client. In both, the
paper doesn't decide it: CRA and the courts look at the real relationship (see the contractor-vs-
employee check in the lawyer skill). A contractor agreement for someone who works like an employee
doesn't protect the business.

| Clause | Normal | Red flag | Ask for / use (paste-ready) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relationship | States independent contractor; contractor controls how and when, uses own tools, can work for others | Set hours, owner's tools, exclusivity, supervision (these point to employment whatever the label) | "The Contractor decides how, when and where the Services are performed, provides their own tools and equipment, and may provide services to others." |
| Taxes and remittances | Contractor responsible for own income tax, CPP and GST/HST; invoices with GST/HST number if registered | Payer withholds tax (looks like employment) | "The Contractor is responsible for their own income tax, Canada Pension Plan contributions and GST/HST. The Contractor will invoice monthly and include their GST/HST number if registered." |
| Workers' compensation | Contractor has their own coverage, or confirms they're exempt | Silence (in some provinces and trades the payer can owe premiums for uncovered contractors) | "The Contractor will provide a clearance certificate from [WCB/WSIB/WorkSafeBC/CNESST] or confirm in writing that they are not required to register." |
| Deliverables and acceptance | Clear deliverables, deadlines, acceptance window | "As directed" | See service agreement Scope. |
| Payment | Per invoice, net 15 to 30; T4A issued for fees over $500 for services | Payment only when the end client pays (pay-when-paid) with no backstop | "Invoices are payable within [30] days of receipt, whether or not the Company has been paid by its own client." |
| IP | Company owns deliverables once paid; contractor waives moral rights in them | Contractor keeps everything, or Company takes contractor's pre-existing tools | "On payment, the Contractor assigns to the Company all rights in the deliverables and waives moral rights in them. The Contractor keeps pre-existing materials and grants the Company a perpetual licence to use any included in the deliverables." |
| Confidentiality and privacy | Mutual, survives the end of the agreement | None, when the contractor sees client or patient data | "The Contractor will keep the Company's and its clients' information confidential, use it only for the Services, and return or delete it when the Services end." |
| Non-solicit | 6 to 12 months, the Company's clients the contractor worked with | Non-compete banning work in the field | "For [12] months after the agreement ends, the Contractor will not solicit the Company's clients that the Contractor served under this agreement." |
| Insurance | Contractor carries liability insurance suited to the work | None for a risky trade | "The Contractor will carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $[2,000,000] and provide a certificate on request." |
| Termination | Either side on short notice; pay for work done | None | "Either party may end this agreement on [14] days' written notice. The Company will pay for Services performed to that date." |

Year-end: issue a T4A to each contractor paid more than $500 for services in the year (due the last day
of February). Put that date in `memory/promises.md` when a contractor is added.
