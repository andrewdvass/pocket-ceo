# NDA (confidentiality agreement): clause library

Most NDAs are routine. Check four things: is it mutual, how is "confidential" defined, how long does it
last, and does it sneak in anything that isn't about confidentiality.

| Clause | Normal | Red flag | Ask for (paste-ready) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mutual or one-way | Mutual when both sides share information; one-way is fine if only they share | One-way against you when you're the one sharing | "Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and each party's obligations apply equally." |
| Definition | Information marked confidential or that a reasonable person would see as confidential | "All information of any kind, whether or not confidential" | "Confidential Information does not include information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was already known to it, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without a duty of confidence." |
| Use | Only to evaluate or perform the deal | Recipient may use it for any purpose | "The recipient will use Confidential Information only to [evaluate / perform] the [project]." |
| Term | 2 to 5 years; trade secrets as long as they stay secret | Forever, for all information | "These obligations last [three (3)] years after the last disclosure, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets." |
| Required disclosure | Allowed if a court or law requires it, with notice | No exception | "The recipient may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, after giving the discloser prompt notice where legally allowed." |
| Return or destroy | On request, with a copy allowed for legal records and backups | Certify destruction of every backup immediately | "On request, the recipient will return or destroy Confidential Information, except copies kept in routine backups or as required by law, which remain confidential." |
| Hidden extras | None | Non-compete, non-solicit, IP assignment, exclusivity, or an indemnity inside an NDA | Delete them: "This Agreement deals only with confidentiality." |
| Remedies | Injunction allowed | Fixed "liquidated damages" (e.g. $50,000 per breach) | Delete the fixed amount. |
| Governing law | Your province | Foreign courts | "Governed by the laws of [province]." |
