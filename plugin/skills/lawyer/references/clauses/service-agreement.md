# Client service agreement: clause library

For the owner's own contract with their clients (or a client's contract they've been asked to sign).
Goal: get paid, keep scope from creeping, keep your own tools and know-how, cap the downside.

| Clause | Normal | Red flag (rate High) | Ask for / use (paste-ready) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Deliverables listed, with what's NOT included and how changes are priced | "Services as requested by the Client" with a fixed price | "Work outside the Scope in Schedule A will be quoted separately and done only after the Client approves the quote in writing (email is fine)." |
| Price and taxes | Price plus applicable taxes | Price "inclusive of all taxes" when you charge GST/HST (you'd eat 5 to 15%) | "Fees are in Canadian dollars and do not include GST/HST or other applicable taxes, which will be added to each invoice." |
| Payment | Deposit for new clients; invoices due in 15 to 30 days | Payment only on "final acceptance" with no deadline to accept | "Invoices are due within [15] days. A [50]% deposit is due before work starts. Deliverables are deemed accepted [10] days after delivery unless the Client gives written reasons why they don't meet Schedule A." |
| Late payment | Interest on overdue amounts, right to pause work | None | "Overdue amounts bear interest at [1.5]% per month ([18]% per year). The Service Provider may pause work while any invoice is more than [15] days overdue." (Interest must be stated as a yearly rate too under the federal Interest Act.) |
| Intellectual property | Client owns the final deliverables once paid; you keep your pre-existing tools, templates and know-how | All IP "created or used" transfers, including your templates; transfer happens before payment | "On payment in full, the Client owns the final deliverables. The Service Provider keeps ownership of its pre-existing materials, tools and templates, and grants the Client a perpetual licence to use any of them included in the deliverables. The Service Provider may show the work in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing." |
| Liability cap | Capped at fees paid in the last 12 months | Unlimited, or including the client's lost profits | "Each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses, including lost profits. These limits don't apply to fraud, wilful misconduct or breach of confidentiality." |
| Indemnity | Each side covers losses from its own breach or negligence | You indemnify the client for "any and all claims arising from the services" | "Each party will indemnify the other for third-party claims to the extent caused by its own breach, negligence or wilful misconduct." |
| Termination | Either side on 30 days' notice; you're paid for work done | Client can cancel any time and pay nothing for work in progress | "Either party may terminate on [30] days' written notice. The Client will pay for all work done and expenses incurred up to the termination date." |
| Non-solicit | Short (6 to 12 months), limited to staff you introduced | Broad non-compete stopping you working in your field | "During the Term and for [12] months after, neither party will solicit for employment the other's employees who worked on the Services." |
| Governing law | Your province | A province or country far away | "This Agreement is governed by the laws of [province] and the federal laws of Canada that apply there." |
| Privacy | You follow the applicable privacy law for any client personal information | Silence when you'll handle customer or patient data | "Each party will handle personal information under this Agreement in line with applicable privacy law ([PIPEDA / provincial act]) and will notify the other promptly of any breach involving it." |

Quebec: consumer contracts and many standard-form contracts must be in French unless the client
expressly asks for English; check before sending a template to a Quebec client.
