# Supplier and vendor terms: clause library

Software subscriptions, equipment leases, merchant services and wholesale supply agreements. These are
usually take-it-or-leave-it, but the auto-renewal and cancellation terms are where small businesses
lose money, so always find them.

| Clause | Normal | Red flag (rate High when money is real) | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Term and auto-renewal | Monthly, or yearly with renewal reminder | Auto-renews for a full new term unless cancelled 60 to 90 days before the end | Put the cancellation deadline in `memory/promises.md` now, with a reminder 2 weeks before. Ask: "Please change renewal to month-to-month after the initial term." |
| Price changes | Fixed for the term, notice before renewal increases | Supplier can raise prices any time | "Prices are fixed for the initial term. Any increase on renewal requires [60] days' written notice." |
| Early cancellation | Pay to the end of the month | Pay the whole remaining term, plus fees (common in equipment leases and merchant services) | Know the exit cost before signing; ask for a cap: "Early termination fees will not exceed [three (3)] months of charges." |
| Equipment leases | Fair market value buyout | Non-cancellable, "hell or high water" clause, $1 buyout hidden as large residual | Compare the total of all payments with the purchase price; flag it if more than about 1.3x for a 3-year lease. |
| Merchant processing | Rate, monthly fee, statement fee; no lock-in | Multi-year term, liquidated damages for leaving, PCI non-compliance fees | Ask for the effective rate on last month's sales and the total exit cost. |
| Warranty and liability | Basic warranty; supplier liability capped at fees | No warranty and no recourse if they lose your data | "The Supplier will maintain backups of Customer data and restore it at no charge if lost through the Supplier's fault." |
| Data and privacy | Your data stays yours, exportable on exit | Supplier owns or can resell your customer data | "The Customer owns its data. On termination the Supplier will provide a full export in a standard format within [30] days." |
| Minimum purchase | None or realistic | Minimum order commitments with shortfall penalties | Lower or delete the minimum. |
| Personal guarantee | Rare for subscriptions; common for equipment financing | Unlimited guarantee by the owner | Cap it to the equipment value, or decline. |
