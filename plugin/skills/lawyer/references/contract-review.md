# Contract review checklist

Read the whole document once, then check these in order. Only report what matters.

## Always check (the money and the exit)
1. **Parties:** is the right legal name used (their business name, "Inc." if incorporated)? Signing
   personally when they have a corporation makes them personally liable.
2. **Scope:** exactly what each side must deliver. Vague scope is the #1 source of disputes.
3. **Price and payment:** amount, due dates, late fees, who pays taxes (GST/HST "plus applicable taxes").
4. **Term and renewal:** how long; does it **auto-renew**, and how much notice to stop it?
5. **Termination:** can they get out, with how much notice, and what does it cost?
6. **Liability:** is there a cap? Is the owner's liability unlimited while the other side's is capped?
   A fair default: each side's liability capped at the fees paid in the last 12 months, with standard
   exceptions (fraud, gross negligence).
7. **Indemnity:** does the owner promise to cover the other side's losses broadly? Narrow it to losses
   caused by the owner's own breach or negligence.
8. **Personal guarantee:** a commercial lease or loan asking the owner to personally guarantee is a
   High item. Negotiate a cap or time limit.

## Check when relevant
- **Non-compete / non-solicit:** non-competes are often hard to enforce and Ontario bans most
  non-competes for employees; check the province. Keep non-solicits short and narrow.
- **Intellectual property:** who owns the work product? Owners usually want to keep their templates,
  methods and pre-existing material, and license or assign only the custom deliverables.
- **Confidentiality:** mutual is normal; perpetual for trade secrets, 2–5 years otherwise.
- **Exclusivity:** are they locked out of other clients/suppliers?
- **Governing law and disputes:** their own province is best. Out-of-province courts raise the cost of
  any dispute; arbitration clauses can be fine or expensive depending on the rules named.
- **Privacy and data:** if personal or health information is handled, check that the contract covers
  storage, access and breach notice in line with the applicable privacy law.
- **Assignment:** can the other side transfer the contract to someone else without consent?
- **Payment terms on the owner's own client contracts:** deposits, refund policy, chargebacks.

## Red flags (rate High)
Unlimited personal liability, personal guarantees without a cap, auto-renewal with a long notice
window, one-sided termination, giving away all IP including pre-existing material, penalties far above
real losses, and blank or "to be agreed" price terms.

## Suggested wording
Whenever you flag a clause, give replacement text they can paste. Keep it short and normal-sounding.
Example (liability cap): "Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees
paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim, except for fraud, wilful misconduct, or breach of
confidentiality."
