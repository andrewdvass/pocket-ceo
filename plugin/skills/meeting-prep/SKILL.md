# Meeting prep

Walk in knowing more than the other side expects. Follow `../../shared/rules.md`.

## Gather (in this order)
1. **What we already know**: search `memory/` (people, customers, promises, logs) and `decisions.md`
   for the person and their company. Open invoices (`invoices aging`), past emails (Gmail search by name
   or domain, read-only), last meeting notes.
2. **Mutual connections**: people in `memory/people.md` who work with, know, or were introduced by them.
   "You both know Dave Patel (your hockey friend; he was at Northwind until 2024)."
3. **Public, professional information** (web search, cite each source): their role and how long
   they've been there, company size and recent news (funding, new location, hiring, awards), what they
   post publicly about (LinkedIn, interviews, podcasts, company blog), and interests they talk about in
   public (a marathon they posted about, the charity board they sit on, their alma mater).
   Use only what they've made public in a professional context. Never home address, family members,
   health, finances or anything that would feel like surveillance if they knew you'd looked.
4. **Their likely problem**: from their industry and company news, what they probably need from the
   owner, and the objection they're likely to raise.

## The brief (one phone screen)
```
Lisa Park, Practice Owner, Brightside Dental (2 clinics, Calgary) · Tomorrow 10:00
You know: met at the BNI lunch Oct 8; she asked about patient reminders. No invoices yet.
Common ground: she runs marathons (posted Calgary Marathon finish, May 2026); you both know Dave Patel.
Company news: opening a 3rd clinic in Airdrie this spring (Calgary Herald, Oct 2026), hiring 2 hygienists.
What she likely needs: fill the new clinic's schedule fast. Likely objection: "we tried an agency before."
Goal for the meeting: agree on a paid pilot for the Airdrie opening.
Open with: congratulate her on Airdrie; ask how she's planning to fill the books.
Sources: brightsidedental.ca, LinkedIn post (May 2026), Calgary Herald (Oct 12, 2026)
```
Mark anything you couldn't confirm as "unconfirmed". If there's almost nothing public, say so and give
three good questions to ask instead.

## After the meeting
Ask in the debrief how it went; save outcomes, next steps and promises; draft the follow-up email.
